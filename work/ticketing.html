SCUFFPay, SCUFFAccess, and SCUFF Ticketing all run at 0€ — no monthly costs, no withdrawal fees, no per-ticket cuts. You publish events, sell tickets, scan the door, and cash out… entirely free.

Why? Because SCUFF doesn’t take money from your base price. SCUFF makes money when the city moves. When demand spikes, SCUFFSurge lifts the ticket price and we take a slice of the uplift, not your revenue.


So your floor price stays yours, your crowds move faster with frictionless entry (QR + native NFC with Apple x SCUFF), and your payments land clean with no deductions.

The more energy your night generates, the more surge pays for everything — keeping your entire stack free.
