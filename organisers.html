Start with your
next event.

The date, the venue, the tickets. Those are the details you start with in SCUFF Partners.

Create an event, add your ticket types and set their prices and availability. Once it’s published, share the event page so your guests can buy their tickets.

As orders come in, you can follow your ticket sales and manage your guest lists. When the doors open, your team can scan tickets and check in guests.

That’s the idea: event setup, ticket sales and entry, in one place.

A few practical questions.

Can I create different ticket types?
Yes. You can set a price and capacity for each ticket type in your event.
What about guest lists?
You can create and manage guest lists for each event in SCUFF Partners.
How do we check tickets at the door?
Your door team can scan ticket QR codes and check in guests using SCUFF’s entry tools.
Can I see how ticket sales are going?
Yes. Your event’s sales and orders are available in SCUFF Partners.
What does it cost?
Contact the team to go through pricing and payment terms for your venue or event.
I already sell tickets elsewhere. Where do I start?
Tell us how you currently manage your events and what you need. We can discuss your setup.
